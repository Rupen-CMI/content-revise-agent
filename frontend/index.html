            Sections rewritten as direct-answer questions
            
              Common market questions drafted for this market (up to three). Each one replaces its section's ordinary heading — the section still covers everything it normally would, but opens with a direct 1-3 line answer to the question instead of a topic sentence.
              
            
                
          
                
        
                
        
                
          
                
            Fact sheet
            
                Every figure the article may state, read off the confirmed report. A missing market size, forecast or growth rate is highlighted as a gap — Step 2 then looks for one outside source to fill it (below, "Figure gap"); a figure that stays a gap is never stated anywhere in the article.
                
            
                  
          
                  
        
                  
        
                  
          
                  
            Most searched questions
            
                  What people actually search about this market. The 2-3 that become article headings are picked when the article is planned (Step 4); the rest are answered in the FAQ. Answerability is checked in Step 3, once the evidence is in.

            Market facts from web search
            
                    Recent news about the confirmed market (not from the CMI report). They are used in the market half of the article. Each fact shows where it came from.
                    
            
                      
          
                      
        
                      
        
                      
          
                      
            Backing for your article's own claims
            
                      Outside sources chosen for each claim from Step 1, with what each one does to the claim: supports, qualifies or contradicts it. They are used in the article's own sections. A claim with no backing is softened or dropped by the writer, never stated as fact.
                      
            
                        
          
                        
        
                        
        
                        
          
                        
            Evidence for the question-headed sections
            
                        Outside sources chosen to ground each question's direct answer (chosen in Step 2 — see Step 2 for the questions themselves and which section each replaces). A question with no outside source still gets answered, from the CMI report and the article's own content.
                        
            
                          
          
                          
        
                          
        
                          
          
                          
            Long-tail keywords the writer can use
            
                          Specific search phrases for this market's commercial and research audience (worth 3+ words each, so they rank where the market's head terms do not). The writer is instructed to weave AT MOST THREE of these into the final draft, word-for-word, only inside sentences it would write anyway — never in a heading or the title. It is normal for a few not to be used: a forced one hurts more than a skipped one.
