### (transition) [transition]
Pulling readable text from a website is one slice of a wider paid market for web data. In that market, specialist firms sell the collection itself: they run the software, the servers and the connections, and they deliver clean, structured information to the buyer. Clients buy because gathering web data in-house takes skilled staff and constant upkeep, and because the pages they want change shape without warning. The result is a service business built on steady demand rather than one-off projects.

### How big is the web scraping services market? [report_context]
The web scraping services market was worth USD 480 million in 2025, according to intelmarketresearch.com. The figure measures what buyers spend on services that collect web data for them. The market is forecast to reach USD 910 million by 2034, which works out to growth of 7.3% a year between 2026 and 2034. Steady growth of that kind fits a service that has moved from an occasional experiment to a regular line in company budgets. The size of the market also reflects how many tasks now depend on web data extraction, from price checks to competitor tracking.

### What is changing in the web scraping services market right now? [trends]
Three trends are reshaping the web scraping services market at once, and each one pushes buyers toward more managed ways of collecting data.
- AI-assisted extraction is spreading but is not yet the norm. Apify and The Web Scraping Club's State of Web Scraping 2026 survey, published in January 2026, found that 45.8% of respondents use AI in their scraping workflows while 54.2% do not. Among the same respondents, 66.2% said they plan to try AI-assisted tools, and 33.8% said they have no such plans.
- Anti-bot defences are getting harder to beat. Grepsr reported in April 2026 that blocking systems have grown from simple rules into layered defences that read behaviour, identify browsers and learn from patterns, which makes scraping less predictable and more costly to run, so enterprises are rethinking how they design collection systems.
- Website owners are blocking bots and pushing back legally. A growing number of sites put technical barriers in place during 2026 to keep AI agents off their pages, citing server costs and lost traffic. The Communications of the ACM reported in October 2026 that objecting owners can now invoke anti-circumvention laws, alongside contract, copyright and computer hacking claims.

### Sub-Segment Analysis of Web Scraping Services [subsegment]
Cloud-based delivery carries the clearest opportunities in the web scraping services market, because rented capacity removes the need for a buyer to own and maintain equipment. A customer pays for collection as a service and lets the provider handle servers, scheduling and upkeep. Published prices show how that billing works. AIJourn reported in October 2026 that Firecrawl's Standard plan costs $83 a month for 100,000 credits, while ZenRows' Launch plan costs $58 for 250,000 credits. The comparison also found that one page costs one credit, that JSON extraction adds four credits, and that pages returning an error still consume a credit.

Market research turns collected pages into decisions, which is why it stands out among the uses of web scraping. A research team uses the data to compare prices, watch rivals and read what customers say. The work adds value at the point where raw pages become a finding a manager can act on, and that step is what buyers pay for.

### Which region leads the web scraping services market? [country_us]
North America led the web scraping services market in 2018, when it held a major share thanks to a large number of key players based there. Asia Pacific is expected to be the fastest-growing region. Read together, those two points describe a market that started in one place and is spreading. For a buyer, the practical effect is choice: North America offers a deep bench of established providers, while Asia Pacific is where new capacity and new competition are likely to appear. The regional split in web scraping services market share by region is therefore worth watching over the coming years, because a market that grows fastest in a new region tends to change how services are priced and delivered.

### Key Players in the Web Scraping Services Market [key_players]
The competitive landscape of the web scraping services market looks crowded rather than settled, and the report names Phantombuster, Mozenda, UiPath, Parseur, PilotFish and Diggernaut among its key players. A long list of that kind points to a field split between proxy aggregators and managed extraction platforms, with no single firm dominating. The companies that offer web scraping services range from small specialist tools to large automation vendors, and each one competes on a slightly different mix of price, scale and ease of use.

### Web Scraping Services Market Outlook [conclusion]
The outlook for the web scraping services market points to steady growth rather than a sudden jump. Spending should climb from the figure reported for 2025 toward the forecast for 2034, at a modest pace of a few percent a year. Demand from digital marketing keeps that growth coming, as teams need fresh web data to plan campaigns and watch rivals. Piracy and tighter controls on website access will shape how services are delivered, pushing more work into managed settings where providers absorb the technical strain. A CMI analyst expects the providers that pair reliable collection with clean, AI-ready output to take share, while those selling raw page dumps lose ground.
