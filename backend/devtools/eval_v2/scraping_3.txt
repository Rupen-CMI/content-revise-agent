### How big is the web scraping services market? [report_context]
The market for web scraping services was worth USD 480 million in 2025, according to intelmarketresearch.com, and it is growing at a modest but steady pace. The figure measures what buyers spend on tools and services that collect public web data. The same research puts the market at USD 910 million by 2034, roughly double its 2025 value. Growth runs at a compound annual rate of 7.3% across the years from 2026 to 2034. The size of the web scraping services market therefore stays small next to many software categories, yet it keeps climbing as more companies treat web data extraction as a standing need rather than a one-off project.

### What is changing in the web scraping services market right now? [trends]
Three forces are reshaping how collection gets done, and each one pushes buyers toward more managed, more careful setups.
- AI-assisted extraction is spreading, but it is not yet the norm. Apify and The Web Scraping Club's State of Web Scraping 2026 survey, published in January 2026, found that 54.2% of respondents do not use AI in their scraping workflows, while 45.8% do. Among those surveyed, 66.2% said they plan to try AI-assisted tools, and 33.8% said they have no such plans. The trends in the web scraping services market point to steady adoption rather than a sudden switch.
- Anti-bot defenses are getting harder to beat. Grepsr reported in April 2026 that blocking systems have moved from simple rules to layered defenses that study behavior, fingerprint a browser and lean on machine learning. Collection has become more complex, less predictable and more resource intensive as a result.
- Websites are pushing back, both technically and legally. Browserless reported in January 2026 that tougher anti-bot systems and rising compliance pressure are pushing teams toward real browsers and managed environments. The Communications of the ACM reported in October 2026 that site owners can now invoke anti-circumvention laws, alongside contract, copyright and computer hacking claims, against collectors they object to.

Pricing like this shapes the opportunities in the web scraping services market, because buyers can start small and scale as their needs grow.
